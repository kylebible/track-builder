Track Builder

How to build
  • Tap a piece in the palette to put it down. Its open ends show as dots: blue pegs, orange holes.
  • Tap an open end, then palette pieces, to keep extending the track from it.
  • Drag a piece to move it; drag near an open end and it snaps on. Shift-drag, or double-click then drag, moves a whole section.
  • Flip turns a curve or switch the other way. Swap end attaches a piece by its other end.
  • Drag empty floor to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan.
