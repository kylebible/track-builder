How to build

  • Add Tap a dot on an open end, then tap pieces in the palette. Greyed-out pieces have no end that fits.
  • Drag in Or drag a piece from the palette onto the floor. Drop it near an open end and it joins on.
  • Left or right The palette's Curves turn setting decides which way new curves and switches go. Mirror flips one you've already placed.
  • Bridges The arch bridge goes straight on floor track. The viaduct, suspension, truss and covered bridges sit a level up: build an N ramp up, add the bridge, and come down with an N1.
  • Bendy track Put one down straight, then Bend (B) it a step at a time up to 45°, the way the Curves turn setting says. Shift B straightens it.
  • Up a level Ramps climb 64 mm, and track built on from the top stays up there. Raise and Lower lift whatever is selected a level at a time; supports go under raised track by themselves and show up in the parts list.
  • Fill a gap Tap the dot on an open end, press Fill gap, then tap the end to join it to. Runs of track that fit show up as they're found, however long they need to be; pick one and Use this.
  • Generate Press Generate for a new section: say how many pieces, a run or a loop, how curvy, how many kinds of track, how many switches (and how many of those loop back onto the line), whether it climbs a level, and how many stops and accessories. Stops are picked to suit the jobs already on the layout: a farm gets a market to take its milk, workers get a station. Another rolls a new one; tap an open end first (or while it's open) to build on from there. Finish at: An end… then tap another open end, and the run is joined to it with Fill gap, keeping the switches, climb and stops you asked for.
  • Move Drag a piece; bring it near an open end and it snaps on. Connected (A, or double-click) selects everything joined to it.
  • Select several Shift-click pieces to add them, or Shift-drag a box round them. On a phone, tap Select first. Drag any selected piece to move them all; Rotate, Mirror, Raise and Delete act on them together.
  • Copy Copy and Paste put a copy of the selection down where you point, or Duplicate puts one right beside it. Drag it near an open end to join it on.
  • Scene Build on a play table, a rug, a kids' play mat, the floor, the grid mat, grass, town streets, by a river or lake, or in snow. The check says when track runs off a table or rug, or into water.
  • Templates Start from a simple oval, a figure eight, passing loops, a goods yard or a hill, under Save & share or on the empty table. Undo brings back what you had.
  • Look around Drag the floor to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan.
