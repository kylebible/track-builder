How to build

  • Add Tap a dot on an open end, then tap pieces in the palette. Greyed-out pieces have no end that fits.
  • Drag in Or drag a piece from the palette onto the floor. Drop it near an open end and it joins on.
  • Left or right The palette's Curves turn setting decides which way new curves and switches go. Mirror flips one you've already placed.
  • Move Drag a piece; bring it near an open end and it snaps on. Shift-drag moves everything joined to it.
  • Scene Build on a play table, a rug, the floor, grass or the grid mat. The check says when track runs off a table or rug.
  • Look around Drag the floor to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan.
